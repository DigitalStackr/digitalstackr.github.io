How it works

  • Pick a problem you already understand. Boring and specific beats clever and broad.
  • Build it with free tools. Canva or Notion is enough. It does not need to be pretty, it needs to be useful.
  • Put it on Gumroad. They handle checkout, delivery and refunds so you don't have to.
  • Post about the problem daily. Not the product — the problem it solves.
  • Keep going through the quiet part. This is where almost everyone stops.

Common questions

Do I need an audience first?

No. Most of our own sales come from people who saw a single post, looked at the profile, and bought within about ten minutes. They never followed first.

Do I need to show my face?

No. Everything we do is faceless. People buy the outcome, not the person — they just need to believe you have actually done the thing.

How much does it cost to start?

Nothing beyond the guide itself. Canva, Gumroad, Beacons and Threads all have free tiers that are enough to launch.

Are refunds available?

Yes. Everything is sold through Gumroad, which handles payment and refunds directly. Use the refund option on your receipt email.

Is this a get-rich-quick thing?

No, and we would rather say so plainly. It is a product, an audience you build slowly, and a lot of repetition. Most weeks are quiet.
