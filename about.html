About

DigitalStackr is a small, one-person digital products business. It started in February 2026 with one file, no audience and no money to spend.

The short version

I'm 19. I built this from a phone because that's what I had. There was no startup capital, no team, and no backup plan — which is mostly why I didn't stop when nothing was happening for the first couple of months.

The first product took a few days to write and sold nothing for weeks. It now sells most days without me touching it. That gap between building the thing and the thing working is the part nobody talks about, so we talk about it a lot.

Why faceless

Nothing we publish shows a face. Partly preference, partly because it removes a real barrier: you don't need to be comfortable on camera to make something useful and sell it. People buy the outcome. The evidence that matters is whether the thing works, which is what the screenshots are for.

How we talk about numbers

We publish real screenshots, including bad weeks. We don't promise you a daily figure, because we can't know it and nobody who claims to does either.

If you ever see a number from us that doesn't add up, tell us — that matters more to this business than any individual sale.

Where we publish

  • Threads — daily posts, where most of it happens
  • Instagram & Facebook — short-form video
  • Telegram — longer breakdowns, a few times a week
  • X — shorter takes
